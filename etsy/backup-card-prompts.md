# Backup card prompts: Christmas After Dark style

Every prompt follows the rules that worked for the Window and Rooftops cards:
- `--ar 16:9` so one image makes both the laptop card and the phone card
- the main subject in the CENTRE, so the phone card's middle slice keeps it
- a warm light source (lamp, candle, window, lantern) for the flicker
- no lettering (`--no text, letters, signature, watermark`)
- your style codes: `--sref 7065184119 4662839742 --profile qb9q1py --sw 20`

"Snow" says where Claude puts the falling-snow effect.
