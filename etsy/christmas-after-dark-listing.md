HOW IT WORKS
This is a DIGITAL DOWNLOAD. Nothing is shipped.

HOW TO PUT IT ON YOUR FRAME TV
1. Download the TV images to your phone.
2. Open the SmartThings app and choose your Frame TV.
3. Tap Art Mode, then Add Your Photos, and pick an image.

HOW TO SET YOUR PHONE WALLPAPER
iPhone: Photos → choose the image → Share → Use as Wallpaper.
Android: long-press your home screen → Wallpaper → choose the image.
