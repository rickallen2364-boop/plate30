# Midnight Joe Art: Christmas advertising plan

**Goal:** get people who have never heard of MidnightJoeArt onto your Etsy listings.

**Main idea:** your product is a moving card, and moving pictures are what TikTok, Reels, Pinterest and Facebook push hardest. The card videos ARE the ads.

**One link everywhere:** a short link to your Etsy shop, e.g. `bit.ly/midnightjoeart` (Claude can make this), plus a QR code version.

---

## 1. Free: the card advertises itself (start now)

Every card a buyer sends is seen by their friends and family. Right now the card only says "MidnightJoeArt" in tiny letters.
- [ ] Ask Claude to change the small signature on the inside page to **"MidnightJoeArt · on Etsy"**, so every card sent is a free ad
- [ ] Send the cards to **your own** friends, family and church/work contacts this week, with the message: *"I made these animated Christmas cards. If you'd like to send them too, they're in my Etsy shop: [link]"*
- [ ] Ask the first few buyers you know personally to **leave an honest review**. Reviews matter more than anything for strangers.

## 2. Free: short videos, 1 a day (biggest reach)

Post the **same video** to all four places. Each one takes about 5 minutes.

| Platform | Account | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Business account, Etsy link in bio | Can reach strangers on day one, even with 0 followers |
| **Instagram Reels** | Business account, link in bio | Same video; older buyers who send cards |
| **Facebook Reels** + your own page | Facebook Page "MidnightJoeArt" | The main card-buying age group |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Your Midnight Joe Art channel | Grows the channel for the long videos |

**15 video ideas** (Claude makes the clips with text on screen):
1. "POV: you open the prettiest Christmas card" (envelope opening)
2. "Too busy to mail cards this year? Send these instead"
3. Still painting → comes alive (before and after)
4. "Which one would you send? 1, 2, 3 or 4?" (comments push videos)
5. "I painted a quiet snowy town, then made the lights flicker"
6. The candle card, close up, flame flickering, calm music
7. "Christmas card for your co-workers" (Rush Christmas Eve)
8. "My TV this Christmas" (painting on a TV, cozy room)
9. Slow zoom across the Winter Rooftops painting
10. "Send this to someone you miss" (Frosted Window)
11. "How I make animated Christmas cards" (Midjourney → flicker → card)
12. "16 cards, $29, send to as many people as you like"
13. "Last minute? These arrive in seconds" (Dec 12–24)
14. 15 seconds of the relaxing YouTube video: "2 hours on my channel"
15. "Black Friday: 30% off all my Christmas cards" (Nov 27–30)

**Hashtags** (pick 5): #christmascards #animatedcard #digitalchristmascard #ecard #christmasart #cozychristmas #framtvart #samsungframetv #etsyshop #smallbusiness #moodyart #christmasvibes

## 3. Free: Pinterest (slow, but it lasts)

- [ ] Pinterest Business account, claim your Etsy shop
- [ ] 3–5 pins a day: each card as a **video pin**, each painting as a **tall image pin** (Claude makes them)
- [ ] Pin titles use search words: *"Animated Christmas Card – Digital E-Card to Text or Email"*
- Pins take weeks to pick up, so start now; many will keep working next Christmas too

## 4. Free: places people already gather

Read each group's rules first. Many only allow selling on certain days.
- **Facebook groups:** Samsung Frame TV owners groups (for the TV art), Christmas decorating groups, your local community / buy-and-sell groups (for cards)
- **Reddit:** r/FrameTV allows sharing art on some days; check its rules before posting
- **Your local church, club or workplace newsletter:** "Local artist's animated Christmas cards"

## 5. Paid: small and controlled (only once listings are live)

| Where | Budget | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Etsy Offsite Ads** | $0 upfront | Leave it ON | Etsy pays to show your listings on Google, Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest; you only pay a fee (15%) when one of those ads leads to a sale |
| **Etsy Ads** | $1–3 a day | From listing day to Dec 15 | Shows your cards higher in Etsy search while you have no reviews |
| **Boost your best TikTok or Instagram Reel** | $5–10 a day | 3–5 days around Black Friday (Nov 26–30) and Dec 8–12 | Only boost a video that already did well for free |

**Total if you do all of it:** about **$80–150 for the whole season.** Stop any paid ad that gets views but no Etsy visits after 3 days. Check in Etsy's **Stats** page where visitors come from.

## 6. Weekly rhythm (about 30 minutes a day)

- **Every day:** 1 video to TikTok, Reels, Facebook and Shorts · 3–5 Pinterest pins · answer messages and comments
- **Twice a week:** share in 1–2 Facebook groups (within their rules)
- **Every Sunday:** check Etsy Stats and tell Claude which videos and listings did best; do more of those

## Dates
- **Nov 14–20:** listings live · accounts set up · send cards to everyone you know
- **Nov 26–30:** Black Friday–Cyber Monday 30% sale · boosted video
- **Dec 1–11:** peak card weeks · post the card videos daily
- **Dec 12–15:** "last minute, sends in seconds"
- **Dec 16–24:** TV art and YouTube: "on your TV tonight"
